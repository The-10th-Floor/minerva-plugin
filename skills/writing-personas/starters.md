# Starter personas

Nine starters ship with Minerva, three per starter set. `personas_list` shows the ones this workspace has (they carry a `templateId`); the others are added in the app (Library, New, from a template). Arabic names are given in English here.

| Starter (set) | Dialect, look | Difficulty | Distinctive controls | Practices |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rushed customer whose order is late (customer service) | Jordanian Arabic, woman 25–34 | 3 | patience 2, decisiveness 4, irritated, escalates after 5 | A concrete fix and time, fast |
| Polite regular with a wrong order (customer service) | US English, man 35–44 | 2 | politeness 5, calm, escalates after 8 | Serving a polite caller fully |
| Angry customer charged twice (customer service) | Gulf Arabic, man 45–54 | 4 | patience 1, skepticism 5, interrupts often, escalates after 3 | Owning a repeat complaint |
| Impatient power user who tried everything (technical support) | US English, man 25–34 | 4 | patience 1, rushed, interrupts often | Skipping the script for an expert |
| Older customer who needs simple steps (technical support) | Jordanian Arabic, woman 65+ | 2 | patience 4, confused, escalates after 9 | Plain steps, checking each one |
| Account owner locked out before a deadline (technical support) | US English, woman 35–44 | 3 | decisiveness 5, rushed | Verification under time pressure |
| Busy salon owner who dislikes cold calls (sales) | Jordanian Arabic, woman 35–44 | 4 | skepticism 5, rushed, interrupts often | Earning the first seconds of a cold call |
| Skeptical finance lead with a competing quote (sales) | US English, man 45–54 | 3 | skepticism 5, calm | Defending value on price |
| Friendly restaurant owner who postpones every decision (sales) | Gulf Arabic, man 45–54 | 3 | talkativeness 5, decisiveness 1, friendly | Getting a real commitment |

`docs_get personas` ends with one of these, "Polite regular with a wrong order", in the exact shape `personas_save` takes.
